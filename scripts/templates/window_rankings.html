Drug rankings by timepoint window

Every drug is shown with the window its reversal score came from. The pipeline otherwise averages 6 h and 24 h into one number, which can match neither: clomifene in BRCA-LumA reads 98.0 early and 12.9 late, and pools to 46.7.

Read the flags with the scores. target absent means every curated mechanism target is unexpressed in that disease — a high reversal percentile beside it is a suppressed false positive, not a lead. Early and late percentiles are computed over different populations, so they compare as rankings, not magnitudes, and n (conditions behind each window) is usually 1 — treat a large gap as a flag for inspection, not an effect size.
