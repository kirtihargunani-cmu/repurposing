QUESTION

R² 0.875Median held-out accuracy of inferred L1000 genes

No. Typical inferred gene values were accurately reconstructed; the narrow failure arises when using the perturbed target’s own imputed transcript as knockout/knockdown verification. The imputer cannot observe a break from the usual gene co-variation.

NEW OUTPUT CONTRACT

Keep the measurements. Remove the final suitability score.

The unit of output becomes a structured mechanism trace. For every drug × target unit × disease subgroup, each link includes its expected claim, observed evidence, provenance, independence, concentration, alternative explanations, and a verdict: holds / weak / breaks / not evaluable. A broken or unevaluable link cannot be averaged away.

L1Drug → binding target
L2Target → disease driver evidence
L3Target → regulators
L4Regulators → genes
L5Prediction → response
L6Concentration & confounds
L7Disease closure

New first-class records: patient-level disease-target prevalence and driver evidence (alteration and functional state, not expression alone); and typed drug-target pharmacology (Ki/Kd vs IC50/EC50, action, selectivity, free exposure, applicability). Numerical values stay inspectable inside a link. No blended 0–100 suitability score is presented as mechanistic truth.

Next falsifiable milestone

Complete L5–L7 on the already frozen fulvestrant→ESR1 and vemurafenib→BRAF predictions, without changing expected signs after seeing the response. Evaluate all five negative-control classes before reporting a convincing trace:

  • Target absent in the tested line.
  • RAF inhibitor in a BRAF-wild-type / RAS-active context.
  • Random or inappropriate drug–cohort pairing.
  • Generic cytotoxic transcriptional response.
  • Drug target lacking an evaluable anchored TF route.

Success is not “both traces close.” Success is a trace procedure that identifies exactly where a claim fails, keeps alternative explanations alive, and reports the rate at which negative controls incorrectly close.
