Confounder 1 · LINCS time resolution

Pooling 6 h and 24 h averages two different responses

  • LINCS L1000 reads 978 landmark genes after drug perturbation, across doses, at 6 h, 24 h, or an unrecorded time.
  • Early (≤6 h) response tracks direct mechanism; late (≥24 h) tracks phenotype (De Wolf 2016). They are not interchangeable.
  • A pooled profile averages the two — which dilutes the signal and, when the two windows disagree in sign, inverts it.
  • Measured worst case: anti-estrogens over the ESR1 program in MCF7 read correct at 6 h but inverted at 24 h, and the late window outvotes the early one roughly 10 : 1.
