Validation 1 · Per-timepoint activity floor

Calibrating the activity (TAS) floor per timepoint

  • A signature was kept if TAS ≥ 0.4, applied uniformly. It was fixed by decision (9 Oct) with no sweep, no derivation.
  • TAS measures response magnitude, which genuinely rises with time; reproducibility is handled separately. A flat floor demands a 6 h response be as big as a 24 h one.
  • Cost: 13,313 cells had 6 h data that was discarded — 76% of 6 h signatures, clustered just under 0.4.
  • Calibrated with a label-blind test: each 6 h signature's drug-specific signal, δ = r(own 24 h) − r(other drugs). Signal degrades gracefully, not off a cliff.
  • Adopted: 6 h floor 0.25 (keeps 75% of 6 h signatures vs 42%); 24 h stays 0.40.

Rigor notes worth saying aloud: the procedure is pre-registered, the floor is reported as a profile across anchor quantiles rather than a single tuned value, and one originally-planned guard (79n) was withdrawn when I found it never reads a compound signature and so could not have detected admitted noise — it was replaced with the drug-specific-signal hard-fail shown here. 24 h is held at 0.40 as the status-quo anchor; only the early window had the signal measurement that justifies lowering it.
