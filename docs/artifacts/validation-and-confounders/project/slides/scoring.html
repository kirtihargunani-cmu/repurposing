Open question · Scoring & validation

The scoring metric and its validation remain open

  • Tried cosine similarity across several information levels — contribution cosine (null), program score (null), window-separated nested cosine, plus production rev / rev_gene / layered_pct.
  • Reparameterizing the vectors is not where a gain lives (contribution vs raw-z cosine agree at Spearman 0.94). The live axes are time and gene-level detail.
  • Deeper confound, literature-backed: reversal largely reflects generic anti-proliferation (Koudijs 2023; Szalai 2019). The proliferation split helps; disease-specific signal beyond it is not yet shown.
  • The binding gap is ground truth: IC50, PRISM and indication lists all reward anti-proliferation. Next: PANACEA, an activity-space benchmark independent of GDSC.

The point I want to land with the PI: the open problem is not only "which similarity function" — it's that every ground truth we have rewards the generic anti-proliferation confound, so a metric that scores better on those truths may just be a better cytotoxicity detector. PANACEA is attractive because it tests the activity-space step directly (does VIPER on a drug profile recover the drug's known mechanism) and is independent of GDSC, so it does not inherit the circularity that rules GDSC out for us.
