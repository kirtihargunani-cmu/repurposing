Validation 3 · Gene-level information

Regulator NES alone is lossy; keeping gene-level detail

  • Two drugs can earn the same regulator NES while perturbing different genes inside the regulon. A per-regulon score cannot tell them apart — it is lossy.
  • The production score rev multiplies totals — blind to which genes carry a regulator's activity.
  • rev_gene requires the genes to line up with the disease pattern one-by-one. A regulator whose activity sits on off-pattern genes drops out by geometry — no threshold, no tuned parameter.
  • Exactly the intended behaviour: hit disease-relevant genes → counts fully; reach activity through unrelated genes → down-weighted.
  • Enriched, not redundant: rev vs rev_gene correlate at Spearman +0.70 — most of the ranking survives while the ties separate.

rev  =  −Σ (disease total × drug total)  ·  a product of sums

rev_gene  =  −ΣΣ (disease gene · drug gene)  ·  a sum of products

KLF4 is the instructive case: the totals miss it, but its genes are concordant with the disease pattern, so the gene view promotes it; TGIF1 is the opposite, near-orthogonal, so it is demoted. The separation test is registered so that "rev_gene separates tied drugs" only counts if the separation tracks something mechanistic (shared target/MOA), not just noise with structure.
