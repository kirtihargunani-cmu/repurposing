Slide 3 · timepoints

Confounder 1 · LINCS time resolution

Pooling 6 h and 24 h averages two different responses

  • LINCS L1000 reads 978 landmark genes after drug perturbation, across doses, at 6 h, 24 h, or an unrecorded time.
  • Early (≤6 h) response tracks direct mechanism; late (≥24 h) tracks phenotype (De Wolf 2016). They are not interchangeable.
  • A pooled profile averages the two — which dilutes the signal and, when the two windows disagree in sign, inverts it.
  • Measured worst case: anti-estrogens over the ESR1 program in MCF7 read correct at 6 h but inverted at 24 h, and the late window outvotes the early one roughly 10 : 1.

Slide 4 · tasfloor

Validation 1 · Per-timepoint activity floor

Calibrating the activity (TAS) floor per timepoint

  • A signature was kept if TAS ≥ 0.4, applied uniformly. It was fixed by decision (9 Oct) with no sweep, no derivation.
  • TAS measures response magnitude, which genuinely rises with time; reproducibility is handled separately. A flat floor demands a 6 h response be as big as a 24 h one.
  • Cost: 13,313 cells had 6 h data that was discarded — 76% of 6 h signatures, clustered just under 0.4.
  • Calibrated with a label-blind test: each 6 h signature's drug-specific signal, δ = r(own 24 h) − r(other drugs). Signal degrades gracefully, not off a cliff.
  • Adopted: 6 h floor 0.25 (keeps 75% of 6 h signatures vs 42%); 24 h stays 0.40.

Slide 5 · impute

Validation 1, continued · Window imputation

Imputing a missing timepoint window: the overlap is too thin

  • Most drugs are scored in a single window: only 939 drugs (4,128 rows) carry both; 13,332 rows carry one.
  • The target is noisy — the median both-row rests on one condition per window, below any predictor's reach.
  • Train ≠ apply: both-window data spans 14 cohorts; the rows to impute span 29 — extrapolation by construction.
  • "Early ≈ late" is weak (37% sign-discordant), and a continuous curve is impossible — only 196 pairs have ≥2 dose-fittable timepoints.
  • Decision: a pre-registered test with acceptance fixed before fitting; until it clears, a missing window stays unknown, not guessed.

Slide 7 · genelevel

Validation 3 · Gene-level information

Regulator NES alone is lossy; keeping gene-level detail

  • Two drugs can earn the same regulator NES while perturbing different genes inside the regulon. A per-regulon score cannot tell them apart — it is lossy.
  • The production score rev multiplies totals — blind to which genes carry a regulator's activity.
  • rev_gene requires the genes to line up with the disease pattern one-by-one. A regulator whose activity sits on off-pattern genes drops out by geometry — no threshold, no tuned parameter.
  • Exactly the intended behaviour: hit disease-relevant genes → counts fully; reach activity through unrelated genes → down-weighted.
  • Enriched, not redundant: rev vs rev_gene correlate at Spearman +0.70 — most of the ranking survives while the ties separate.

rev  =  −Σ (disease total × drug total)  ·  a product of sums

rev_gene  =  −ΣΣ (disease gene · drug gene)  ·  a sum of products

Slide 8 · scoring

Open question · Scoring & validation

The scoring metric and its validation remain open

  • Tried cosine similarity across several information levels — contribution cosine (null), program score (null), window-separated nested cosine, plus production rev / rev_gene / layered_pct.
  • Reparameterizing the vectors is not where a gain lives (contribution vs raw-z cosine agree at Spearman 0.94). The live axes are time and gene-level detail.
  • Deeper confound, literature-backed: reversal largely reflects generic anti-proliferation (Koudijs 2023; Szalai 2019). The proliferation split helps; disease-specific signal beyond it is not yet shown.
  • The binding gap is ground truth: IC50, PRISM and indication lists all reward anti-proliferation. Next: PANACEA, an activity-space benchmark independent of GDSC.

Slide 9 · takeaways

Summary

What is validated, bounded, and open

Validated

  • VIPER activity readout — robust to the regulon set (Spearman 0.99)
  • Per-timepoint TAS floors: 6 h 0.25, 24 h 0.40
  • Splitting windows removes a sign-inverting confound

Scoped honestly

  • Window imputation — too thin; unknown stays
  • Context-free regulon gene lists — ~half is resampling churn
  • The ARACNe / CARNIVAL causal map — no inhibition encoded

Open & next

  • A scoring metric with gene-level detail (D203 test)
  • PANACEA — an independent activity-space benchmark
  • Run the pre-registered imputation & separation tests

Net result: each step is now validated, bounded, or explicitly deferred, so the scoring question can be addressed on validated footing.
