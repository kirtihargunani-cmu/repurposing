Testing the Biology

In order to confirm scoring, we asked whether each layer it rests on carries real biology. Three layers were tested: exposure time, the network and activity build, and the master-regulator score.

Companion deck: scoring results and their interpretation.

Purpose: establish that the inputs are sound before discussing whether any score ranks well. The ordering is a dependency chain, so a failure at an earlier layer would invalidate the later ones rather than merely weaken them. Scope: all results are from TCGA cohorts scored against LINCS L1000 compound profiles in Celligner-matched cell lines. Nothing here leaves cell lines and tumour expression, and no claim is made about patient response. Framing if asked why validation preceded scoring: four scoring variants have been tested and none clearly outperforms the others on the available yardsticks. That outcome is only interpretable if the inputs are known to be sound, which is what this deck establishes.
