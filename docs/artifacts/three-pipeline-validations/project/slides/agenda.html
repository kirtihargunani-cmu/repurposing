Structure

Three validations, in sequence

rev = −(D · s) / ‖s‖ = −Σi nesi · acti / ‖s‖
D = drug regulator activity · s = disease master-regulator NES · i indexes regulators
1

Exposure time

Whether acti should be averaged over timepoints, or resolved by them. Tested: the activity threshold, window concordance, imputability, and whether timing is a drug property.

2

Network and activity inference

Whether the regulons behind nesi and acti encode regulatory structure. Tested: regulon context, knockdown response, exact decomposition, convergent recovery.

3

The summary score

Whether acti determines the gene pattern that produced it. Tested: measurement ceiling, similarity at matched activity, the residual, and the downstream cost.

The sequence is a dependency chain. If exposure time carried no information, averaging over it would be correct and the window arm would be unnecessary complexity. If the networks encoded only co-expression noise, per-cohort regulons would be meaningless and the gene-level decomposition would describe nothing. Only once both hold is it meaningful to ask what the summary discards. Note on notation: nes_i is the tumour-versus-normal enrichment of regulator i, estimated by msviper against GTEx-matched normals. act_i is the drug's inferred activity on the same regulator in cohort-matched cell lines. The normalisation by the norm of s is a per-cohort constant and does not affect within-cohort ranking. If asked why this particular score: rev is the production score and the baseline every variant is compared against. It is unchanged throughout this deck.
