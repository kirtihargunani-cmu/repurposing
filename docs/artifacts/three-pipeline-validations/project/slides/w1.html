The expected direction is negative. Signature counts are 6 at 6 h against 59 at 24 h, so a pooled mean is dominated by the discordant window.

On the threshold: the 0.25 value at 6 h was set from measurement. Over 2,221 six-hour signatures that have a same-drug 24 h reference in the same cell line, drug-specific signal is 0.216 in the 0.250 to 0.284 activity band against 0.364 in the accepted band, with 93.3 per cent of signatures positive. The pass rule holds down to 0.250 and fails below it. No other timepoint has an equivalent measurement, so no other floor was altered. On the ESR1 control: this is a known-answer case rather than a discovery. Anti-estrogens should oppose the ESR1 programme in a luminal line. The early window recovers that direction and the late window reverses it. The ratio of 6 to 59 signatures means that in a plain average the discordant window outweighs the concordant one by roughly an order of magnitude.
