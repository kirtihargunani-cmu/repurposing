Slide 2 · question

The score under examination

Reversal multiplies totals

rev = −(D · s) / ‖s‖ = −Σi nesi · acti / ‖s‖
D = drug regulator activity  ·  s = disease master-regulator NES
  • nesi summarises a whole regulon in one number.
  • acti does the same on the drug side.
  • Their product asks only whether the magnitudes oppose — never whether the same genes are involved.

Two drugs can reach identical activity on a regulator through disjoint sets of its target genes. The score cannot tell them apart.

This is the setup. Everything that follows is about what that collapse costs and whether restoring the gene level recovers anything.

Slide 4 · split

The instrument

Splitting one NES into its genes

t2j = Φ−1(rj)    aj = mj lj t2j    bj = (1 − |mj|) lj t1j
cij = ( aj + σi bj [S2i > 0] ) · ki    Σj cij = the regulator’s score
m = tfmode (signed) · l = likelihood · r = rank over the scored gene universe

Drug side — exact

Condition-level VIPER has no null model, so the per-gene split is the activity. Verified to 4.8×10−7.

Disease side — an allocation

msviper’s permutation null cannot be replayed, so dij = cij · NESi/ESi. Stated wherever d is used.

This decomposition is what makes every later measurement possible. It is arithmetic, not biology — the exactness proves the bookkeeping, nothing more.

Slide 5 · windows

Validation 2 · exposure time

Timepoint is a real dimension — and cannot be imputed

  • A per-timepoint activity floor raises active 6 h signatures from 4,938 to 10,889; a single floor discards 76% of them.
  • 37% of regulator terms flip sign between early and late. For anti-estrogens in MCF7 the ESR1 programme reads −0.056 at 6 h (correct) and +0.094 at 24 h (inverted).
  • Pooling averages a correct reading with an inverted one — the inverted window outvotes it roughly 10 : 1.

Pre-registered, and closed

Best model predicting the missing window: Spearman 0.380.

Simply copying the other window: 0.382.

Sign agreement 0.62–0.63 against a 0.75 bar. Verdict: closed, both directions.

Not a drug property either

A drug’s behaviour in its other cohorts predicts this one at Spearman −0.05. “Drug X is early-acting” is not supported.

About 80% of a window's reliable variance is absent from the other, and discordance does not shrink with more conditions (38.8% to 35.8%). So an unmeasured window is labelled unknown rather than guessed — the empirically correct label, not the cautious one. Coverage limit: 29 of 41 cohorts have window data; 12 have none at all.

Slide 8 · revgene

The correction

A product of sums, or a sum of products

rev      = −Σi (Σj dij)(Σj Cij) / ‖s‖   multiplies the totals
rev_gene = −Σi  Σj dij · Cij    / ‖s‖   pairs the genes, one by one
d = disease gene contributions · C = drug gene contributions · both carry tfmode × likelihood

A regulator whose activity comes from genes orthogonal to the disease pattern has Σj dijCij ≈ 0 even when both totals are large — down-weighted by geometry, with no threshold and no tuned parameter.

Example: demoted

TGIF1 — disease NES +3.52, drug activity −1.17, so reversal scores it high. Gene cosine 0.016: the genes are orthogonal.

Example: promoted

KLF4 — totals say agreement, yet the genes oppose at −0.264. Reversal misses it entirely.

Recorded error: the first version multiplied by minus nes as well, double-counting the disease direction, because d already carries it — sum of d over j equals nes. Caught on the first smoke test when TAL1 read +0.29 by reversal and minus 0.03 by the new score.

Slide 10 · whyauc

The reconciliation

Why this looked like failure for most of the week

  • Every score was judged on approval recovery — do drugs already approved for this cancer rank high?
  • Measured properly, that yardstick is bimodal: 9 of 41 cohorts significantly above chance and 9 significantly below (endometrial at z = −7.7).
  • In the failing cohorts the approved sets are largely cytotoxic, and a single generic proliferation axis beats the mechanism score.

So a score that demotes chemotherapy must lose approval AUC while gaining on target biology. The null result and the positive result are the same fact, measured two ways.

The methodological takeaway: approval recovery is the wrong instrument for a mechanistic claim, and we can now demonstrate that rather than assert it. Biomarker pairs and target-role components are the right anchors.

Slide 12 · didnt

What did not hold

Two ideas closed with numbers

Collapsing regulators into programs

800+ regulators do carry 20–40 dimensional structure, and programs found across patients and drugs recover the same hubs.

But cosine is invariant under rotation, so a program basis cannot change the ranking — and truncation gave no gain at any k.

Programs stay for interpretation, not scoring.

Transferring across cohorts

With identical cell lines but different networks, the same drug’s regulator activity correlates only +0.667.

Cross-tissue: +0.330. Regulon content overlaps at Jaccard 0.06–0.09 even between two breast subtypes.

Regulon redefinition alone costs a third of the signal.

Both were measured before being abandoned, and both are recorded with the number that closed them. The 605 regulators shared by all 41 cohorts are a common vocabulary, not a common measurement.

The cell-line comparison is the clean experiment: BRCA-LumA and BRCA-Basal use the same 69 in-key lines, so the drop from about 1.0 to 0.667 is attributable to the network alone.

Slide 13 · open

Open, and stated plainly

Where the uncertainty actually sits


  • The anchors disagree. Approval recovery says the gene term is worse; target role says it is better. We can explain the disagreement, but not yet adjudicate it.
  • Data bottleneck. Cell-line mapping is fine — median 64 matched lines. But only 41 drugs per cohort have condition-level profiles in those lines, and 15 of 41 cohorts have none.
The pooled per-drug association with dependency is small, Spearman +0.089, while per-cohort gains are much larger. Liver moves the wrong way in both windows. The honest position is that the gene-level term is doing something real and we cannot yet say it is better overall.

Slide 14 · next

Next

Settle the evaluation before the next score

1 · Make resolution first-class

Every row already declares what backs it — window coverage, conditions, lines. Surface it as a confidence tier and stop pooling tiers in evaluation. Keep all 41 cohorts.

2 · Strengthen the biomarker test

It is the one anchor with a mechanistic signal. Expand it, and report it against the permutation null rather than a sign test.

3 · Specify the PRISM / GDSC evaluation

12,926 rows unused. It needs a spec — what the measure means, per-line or per-drug, what the null is — not a merge.

4 · Finish the one unfinished score

Scale-free composition of reversal and the covariance. About an hour. Leaving it open makes four nulls look like five.

5 · Report tiered evidence, not one list

The scores disagree interpretably. Report reversal strength, gene concordance, target dependency and window separately.

6 · Prospective validation

The yardstick problem has no computational fix. A small set of predictions tested in a well-covered cohort converts an unfalsifiable ranking into a falsifiable one.

The line to say out loud: I cannot yet say which scoring variant is better, and I can show why — the two anchors I trust disagree, and the disagreement is explainable. Rather than build a fifth score, settle the evaluation.
