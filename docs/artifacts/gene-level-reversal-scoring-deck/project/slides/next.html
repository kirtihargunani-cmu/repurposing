Settle the evaluation before the next score

1 · Make resolution first-class

Every row already declares what backs it — window coverage, conditions, lines. Surface it as a confidence tier and stop pooling tiers in evaluation. Keep all 41 cohorts.

2 · Strengthen the biomarker test

It is the one anchor with a mechanistic signal. Expand it, and report it against the permutation null rather than a sign test.

3 · Specify the PRISM / GDSC evaluation

12,926 rows unused. It needs a spec — what the measure means, per-line or per-drug, what the null is — not a merge.

4 · Finish the one unfinished score

Scale-free composition of reversal and the covariance. About an hour. Leaving it open makes four nulls look like five.

5 · Report tiered evidence, not one list

The scores disagree interpretably. Report reversal strength, gene concordance, target dependency and window separately.

6 · Prospective validation

The yardstick problem has no computational fix. A small set of predictions tested in a well-covered cohort converts an unfalsifiable ranking into a falsifiable one.

The line to say out loud: I cannot yet say which scoring variant is better, and I can show why — the two anchors I trust disagree, and the disagreement is explainable. Rather than build a fifth score, settle the evaluation.
