The reconciliation

Why this looked like failure for most of the week

  • Every score was judged on approval recovery — do drugs already approved for this cancer rank high?
  • Measured properly, that yardstick is bimodal: 9 of 41 cohorts significantly above chance and 9 significantly below (endometrial at z = −7.7).
  • In the failing cohorts the approved sets are largely cytotoxic, and a single generic proliferation axis beats the mechanism score.

So a score that demotes chemotherapy must lose approval AUC while gaining on target biology. The null result and the positive result are the same fact, measured two ways.

The methodological takeaway: approval recovery is the wrong instrument for a mechanistic claim, and we can now demonstrate that rather than assert it. Biomarker pairs and target-role components are the right anchors.
