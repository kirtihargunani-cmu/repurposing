Where the uncertainty actually sits


  • The anchors disagree. Approval recovery says the gene term is worse; target role says it is better. We can explain the disagreement, but not yet adjudicate it.
  • Data bottleneck. Cell-line mapping is fine — median 64 matched lines. But only 41 drugs per cohort have condition-level profiles in those lines, and 15 of 41 cohorts have none.
The pooled per-drug association with dependency is small, Spearman +0.089, while per-cohort gains are much larger. Liver moves the wrong way in both windows. The honest position is that the gene-level term is doing something real and we cannot yet say it is better overall.
