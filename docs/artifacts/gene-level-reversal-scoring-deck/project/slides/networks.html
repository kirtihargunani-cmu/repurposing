Validation 1 · the builds

The networks carry mechanism

  • Regulon membership is context, not noise: same-cohort Jaccard 0.552 against cross-tissue 0.029.
  • Against LINCS knockdowns — independent of our pipeline — ARACNe reads TF knockdowns at AUC 0.55 (shRNA) and 0.63 (CRISPR).
  • VIPER activity reconstructs exactly from its gene terms: max |difference| 4.8×10−7 over 21,588 drug × cohort × window cells.

The decisive case

ARACNe’s ESR1 regulon reads an ESR1 knockdown correctly.

So when anti-estrogens read as ESR1 activation, that is a real drug effect — GATA3 and FOXA1 rise — not a broken network.

Independent confirmation

Programs clustered across patients and across drugs — unrelated axes — recover the same hubs: PRRX1↔PRRX1 Jaccard 0.71.

The knockdown benchmark is too thin for per-TF regulon choices — 1 to 5 signatures per TF, ARACNe vs CollecTRI paired p = 0.6 — so it is reported and never used to pick regulons.
