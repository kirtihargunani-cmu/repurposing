Timepoint is a real dimension — and cannot be imputed

  • A per-timepoint activity floor raises active 6 h signatures from 4,938 to 10,889; a single floor discards 76% of them.
  • 37% of regulator terms flip sign between early and late. For anti-estrogens in MCF7 the ESR1 programme reads −0.056 at 6 h (correct) and +0.094 at 24 h (inverted).
  • Pooling averages a correct reading with an inverted one — the inverted window outvotes it roughly 10 : 1.

Pre-registered, and closed

Best model predicting the missing window: Spearman 0.380.

Simply copying the other window: 0.382.

Sign agreement 0.62–0.63 against a 0.75 bar. Verdict: closed, both directions.

Not a drug property either

A drug’s behaviour in its other cohorts predicts this one at Spearman −0.05. “Drug X is early-acting” is not supported.

About 80% of a window's reliable variance is absent from the other, and discordance does not shrink with more conditions (38.8% to 35.8%). So an unmeasured window is labelled unknown rather than guessed — the empirically correct label, not the cautious one. Coverage limit: 29 of 41 cohorts have window data; 12 have none at all.
