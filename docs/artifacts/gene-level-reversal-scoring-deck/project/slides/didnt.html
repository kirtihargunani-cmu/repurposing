What did not hold

Two ideas closed with numbers

Collapsing regulators into programs

800+ regulators do carry 20–40 dimensional structure, and programs found across patients and drugs recover the same hubs.

But cosine is invariant under rotation, so a program basis cannot change the ranking — and truncation gave no gain at any k.

Programs stay for interpretation, not scoring.

Transferring across cohorts

With identical cell lines but different networks, the same drug’s regulator activity correlates only +0.667.

Cross-tissue: +0.330. Regulon content overlaps at Jaccard 0.06–0.09 even between two breast subtypes.

Regulon redefinition alone costs a third of the signal.

Both were measured before being abandoned, and both are recorded with the number that closed them. The 605 regulators shared by all 41 cohorts are a common vocabulary, not a common measurement.

The cell-line comparison is the clean experiment: BRCA-LumA and BRCA-Basal use the same 69 in-key lines, so the drop from about 1.0 to 0.667 is attributable to the network alone.
