How the tumor and normal RNA-seq data moved from the raw UCSC Toil recompute to tumor-vs-normal signatures and regulator networks. Every count below is read from the pipeline's result files.

Snapshot 2 Oct 2026repo: kirtihargunani-cmu/repurposingToil RSEM · GENCODE v23

Pipeline

1

Download the Toil recompute

19,131 RNA-seq samples
10,535 TCGA · 734 TARGET · 7,862 GTEx, all reprocessed through one pipeline (RSEM, GENCODE v23). Clinical, subtype, purity and batch tables pulled alongside.
60,498 genes in the matrix before any filtering
2

Define disease cohorts and map them to ontologies and normal tissue

40 cohorts · 33 TCGA + 7 TARGET
Each cohort mapped to MONDO, OncoTree and UBERON (all IDs validated) and matched to a GTEx normal tissue: 12 good, 19 partial, 9 none.
1 cohort has no tumor samples in Toil (TARGET rhabdoid tumor)
10,530 tumor samples across the 39 cohorts with tumors
3

Split mixed cohorts into subtypes, then apply a 30-patient floor

49 disease groups kept · 10,211 patients
9 cohorts split by histology or molecular subtype (BRCA by PAM50, LGG by IDH / 1p19q, SARC, ESCA, CESC, UCEC, PCPG, TGCT, TARGET-ALL by lineage) into 28 subgroups. Groups need at least 30 tumor patients.
+28 subgroups replace 9 mixed cohorts
−9 groups under 30 patients (rare sarcomas, small TARGET cohorts, T-ALL, mixed histologies)
−1 BRCA normal-like (low tumor content)
−21 tumors without a subtype label · −13 male breast tumors (BRCA kept female-only)
4

Keep one sample per patient and build the covariate table

17,746 sample–group rows flagged (one GTEx sample can serve several groups)
10,347 tumors · 737 adjacent normals · 6,662 GTEx normals. Priority: diagnosis sample, then metastasis, then recurrence. Covariates: sex, age, site, batch, ESTIMATE score (15,310 samples), ABSOLUTE purity (90% of TCGA tumors), blast %.
Repeat samples dropped: relapse, metastasis and second-site samples from 169 patients; duplicate GTEx tissues per donor
Melanoma keeps 364 metastatic tumors (no primary available), modeled with a sample-type term
5

Tumor-vs-normal signatures (limma-voom)

41 groups with a signature · 58 contrasts
40 groups vs GTEx normals and 18 vs TCGA adjacent normals (at least 10 normals each). Model adjusts for sex, age, ESTIMATE score and sample type. Agreement between the two normal references: median Spearman about 0.6.
−8 groups have no usable normal: TCGA-LAML, TARGET-AML, B-ALL, DLBC, MESO, THYM, UVM, CHOL (hematologic references parked)
CESC: GTEx vagina pooled for squamous; adenocarcinoma at 7 normals (low power)
6

Map genes to current IDs and build regulator networks

97.4% protein-coding genes mapped to current HGNC
CollecTRI 1,178 regulators (42,601 edges) and DoRothEA A–C 429 (32,286 edges), 99.6% of edges mapped. Project-built ARACNe networks for 28 cohorts from the same Toil data: 6,201 regulators, including 1,633 TFs and 375 kinases.
5,113 GENCODE v23 gene names have been renamed since 2015; mapping goes through Ensembl IDs
ARACNe: 100 bootstraps per network, about 2.5 days of compute
7
Running

Regulator activity per group and per patient (VIPER)

CollecTRI arm now, ARACNe arm when networks finish. Outputs: group master regulators, how well each patient matches the group mechanism, and the main axes of mechanism variation within each group.
